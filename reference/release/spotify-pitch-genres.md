# Spotify Editorial Pitch — Official Genre List

Spotify for Artists' "Pitch a Song" form offers a **closed dropdown** of genres —
no free text. The "Genres (up to 3)" field in a track's `## Distribution & Pitch`
→ "Spotify Editorial Pitch" block (see `/templates/track.md`) must be filled with
values from this list only. Pick the up-to-3 closest matches; don't invent a
genre that isn't here.

Genres are grouped under Spotify's own top-level categories below (a genre can
appear under more than one category, matching Spotify's own overlaps). This is
independent of the DistroKid genre dropdown used for distributor upload — see
`## Genre and Tag Requirements` in this directory's `metadata-by-platform.md`
for that list. The two do not need to match; DistroKid classifies for the
store, this list is for Spotify editorial discovery.
